VERIFICATION POLICY

110% means test
beyond the happy path.

The minimum is an assurance discipline, not a coverage statistic. Every project adds domain-specific verification to the generic harness.

Mandatory verification families

  • Manifest and contract schema validation.
  • Positive, negative, missing, malformed and boundary inputs.
  • Preconditions, postconditions, invariants and state ownership.
  • Independent expected results and reference vectors.
  • Failure, HOLD, timeout, partial-result and recovery behaviour.
  • Mutation testing to demonstrate that tests detect incorrect implementations.
  • Dependency, compatibility and downstream impact analysis.
  • Regression, adversarial and resource-boundary tests appropriate to risk.
  • Reproducibility, source hashes, runtime details and explicit skipped-check reporting.

Risk classes

ClassDefinitionRequired assurance
A · ReplaceableIsolated, bounded effects, no privileged authority.Generic 110% automated checks plus project-specific checks.
B · SignificantImportant workflows, broad dependants or consequential data.Class A plus dependency impact and independent technical review.
C · CriticalTrust kernel, safety, authority, evidence integrity, canonical state or release control.Class B plus complete human examination of the change set and two independent approvals, including a domain-authorised reviewer.

Outcome semantics

  • PASS: all checks required by the generic profile completed successfully.
  • FAIL: at least one check failed. Acceptance is blocked.
  • INCOMPLETE: a required check was skipped or an adopting-project obligation remains. It is not a pass.
  • NOT_APPLICABLE: a check does not apply under the selected generic policy. The adopting project may impose stricter requirements.
Authority is separate from verification. A green workflow does not merge, certify, sign or deploy. Branch protection, CODEOWNERS, reviewer permissions and trusted release workflows must be configured by the repository owner.

Limits

Passing CVAS does not prove total correctness, safety, numerical validity or field fitness. The harness verifies declared contracts and evidence; the adopting project remains responsible for the completeness of requirements, independent references, domain validation and release decisions.
